v0.0.2 · Sandbox
Two teams, ready to inspect
Free movement sandbox. Dice, legal movement, activations, scoring, AI, and leagues are not implemented. Player profiles match the 2025 source. Skill effects are not yet enforced.

Select any player to inspect their profile. Move an active coach’s player by choosing an empty square. Keyboard: Tab to a square and Enter to select it. Passing control changes the active coach.
