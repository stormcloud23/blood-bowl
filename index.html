v0.0.5 · Sandbox
Move actions are now enforced
Move, dodge, rush, jump, stand up, and pick up the ball. Perfect Conditions. This is a practice drive: other actions, full match sequencing, AI, and leagues are still to come.

Changing the setup starts a new practice. Direction template: 1 NW · 2 N · 3 NE · 4 W · 5 E · 6 SW · 7 S · 8 SE.

Select a player to see their movement range. Click a highlighted destination once to preview its route and checks; click the same square again to commit the move through it. After arrival, choose another square if movement remains. Dice are rolled only when committed. Green = no roll, amber = checks required, purple = jump.
