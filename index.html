Movement and ball action practice
Preview movement and its checks, resolve Block dice, pushes, and follow-ups, or secure a loose ball. Perfect Conditions. This remains a practice drive, not a complete match.

Create a team / manage league progression

Changing the mode starts a new game or practice. Direction template: 1 NW · 2 N · 3 NE · 4 W · 5 E · 6 SW · 7 S · 8 SE.

Select a player and choose an action. For movement, click a destination to preview the remaining range from there. Click another highlighted tile to extend the route. Declare the action, then click the selected destination again to commit; Cancel movement preview restores the current range. For Pass or Hand-off, declare the action, move as needed, then choose a ball target. For Blitz, choose Declare Blitz and a highlighted target, move adjacent, then choose Blitz Block (1 MA). During Block, click a highlighted target. Green = no roll on that step; amber = a check on that step or an action target; purple border = jump. Earlier route steps may still require rolls.
