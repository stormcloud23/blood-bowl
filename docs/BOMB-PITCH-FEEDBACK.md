# Bomb range and blast feedback

Choosing **Throw Bomb** in the popup or special-action menu opens an uncommitted
range preview. Click a target once to preview, then repeat the click or choose
**Confirm Throw Bomb** to declare the action and throw. **Cancel preview**,
Escape or clicking the thrower dismisses it without using the player, rolling
dice, consuming the team's bomb allowance or clearing the previous blast marks.
Declaration and any activation-trait/star choices happen only on confirmation.
The confirmed target resumes after those choices once, and mandatory catches,
rethrows and Cindy's second bomb retain their ordinary engine flow.

BB2025 Bombardier prohibits a Move Action before throwing the bomb. The preview
therefore forecasts from the player's current square and does not execute a
planned movement route. A player who has already activated cannot switch to a
Throw Bomb declaration. Source: [Bombardier](https://bloodbowlbase.ru/bb2025/core_rules/skills_and_traits/#bombardier-active).

Throw Bomb targeting uses the same roll-difficulty colors as a normal pass, with
Quick, Short, Long and Long Bomb bands. Difficulty comes from the current bomb
thrower's bomb preview, including native PA, marking, Disturbing Presence,
weather, Accurate, Cannoneer and Nerves of Steel. PA “–” shows an automatic
fumble. These are single-roll targets, without assumed rerolls or catch odds.
If an opponent catches and rethrows a bomb, the colors use their profile.

An explosion leaves a black outline on each on-pitch square in its 3×3 blast
footprint. This records the actual explosion after scatter, bounce or fumble,
rather than the originally aimed target. Adjacent squares belong to the blast
area even when the individual hit roll fails. Multiple explosions in the same
activation, such as Cindy's two bombs, retain their combined footprints.

The outline survives injury decisions, selection changes, rerenders and
turnovers. It clears when the next valid player activation starts, including a
direct Block and an activation that subsequently fails its trait roll. Invalid
activation attempts leave it visible. New games naturally have no blast history.
The outline is a pointer-transparent overlay, so it preserves tokens, pitch
selection and other highlights. This presentation history changes no bomb rules.
