## Hiring and match boundaries

- Maximum two hiring selections and sixteen available participants before
  post-purchase additions. Each pair is one selection and two roster places.
- League CTV purchase order, funding, refunds and atomic postgame accounting
  apply. Removing a paired hire refunds/removes both members together.
- The pinned ALLOW_STAR_ON_BOTH_TEAMS=false option cancels duplicate opposing
  hires and retains both fees. Each participant has a distinct shirt number.
- Stars use normal reserves, setup, dugouts and shared skills. They cannot be
  Team Captain, receive player prayers, win MVP or be permanently recruited.
- Match events can contribute team results, but no permanent star SPP/profile
  changes are saved. Temporary effects and usage counters remain match-local.
- Bugman star and staff purchases are mutually exclusive in both directions.
- Optional decisions are coach-owned and guarded against stale UI callbacks.
