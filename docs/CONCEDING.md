# Conceding

Use Concede game beside New game / reset in Play. Review the consequences, then confirm or keep playing. The active human coach can concede at the start of their turn, before declaring an activation or block. Computer coaches never decide to concede; take over first if needed.

The opponent wins 2–0, or keeps their higher touchdown total while the conceding team's score becomes zero. A concession with penalties removes the conceding team's MVP and winnings, forfeits only SPP earned in this match, and reduces Dedicated Fans by D3 (minimum 1). The winner receives two MVP awards and winnings based on full attendance plus the final touchdown total. Existing SPP, injuries and paid inducement costs remain.

During setup, a team with fewer than three available players can concede without penalty. The same score adjustment applies, with normal post-game MVPs, winnings, SPP and fan updates.

Post-game remains manual. Select the eligible MVP nominees (the winner's pool is rolled twice for a penalized concession) and assign each newly awarded touchdown's SPP to a player of your choice. Team-specific touchdown SPP rules still apply. Save both teams together; the result cannot be applied twice.

The D6 rolls that can cause developed players to leave after a concession are deliberately omitted.
